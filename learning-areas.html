Academic Support Guide

Learning Areas

Practical exploration of core academic domains, observable classroom indicators, and evidence-based supportive strategies.

📖
Domain 01 • Phonological Processing

Reading (Dyslexia)

Challenges with phonetic decoding, word recognition speed, or reading comprehension.

🔍 Observable Indicators (Home & School)
  • Hesitant, strained oral reading or frequent guessing of words.
  • Reversing letters (e.g. b/d, p/q) or omitting word endings.
  • Skipping lines or losing place on the page without a tracking guide.
💡 Evidence-Based Supportive Strategies
  • Structured, multisensory phonics (Orton-Gillingham framework).
  • Audiobooks and text-to-speech tools paired with physical text.
  • Chunking long paragraphs and using increased line spacing.
🩺 When to Seek Consultation

Ongoing difficulty reading at age-expected grade level persisting for 6+ months despite targeted phonics practice.

Domain 02 • Fine Motor & Expression

Writing (Dysgraphia)

Difficulties organizing written expression, fine motor pencil grip, or spatial letter consistency.

🔍 Observable Indicators (Home & School)
  • Inconsistent letter sizing, mixed uppercase/lowercase, and erratic line spacing.
  • Awkward or overly tight pencil grip resulting in rapid hand fatigue.
  • Significant gap between fluent verbal thoughts and labored written output.
💡 Evidence-Based Supportive Strategies
  • Digital typing, speech-to-text dictation, and ergonomic pencil grips.
  • Visual graphic organizers and bulleted outline planning templates.
  • Allowing oral presentations as an alternative assessment format.
🩺 When to Seek Consultation

Written work drastically underrepresents student's verbal intellect, or writing causes physical pain and anxiety.

Domain 03 • Orthographic Memory

Spelling & Word Forms

Challenges retaining orthographic letter patterns, visual word forms, and sound-symbol relationships.

🔍 Observable Indicators (Home & School)
  • Inconsistent spelling of the exact same word multiple times on a single page.
  • Frequent omission or transposition of internal vowels in common words.
  • Extreme reliance on phonetic spelling (e.g. spelling words strictly as they sound).
💡 Evidence-Based Supportive Strategies
  • Multisensory letter tracing using tactile surfaces and color-coded vowels.
  • Teaching word families, morphological roots, prefixes, and suffixes.
  • Using visual mnemonic cue cards and word-prediction spell checkers.
🩺 When to Seek Consultation

Persistent spelling difficulty severely restricting vocabulary choice and undermining student's academic confidence.

💡
Domain 04 • Receptive & Working Memory

Comprehension & Language

Processing complex syntax, inferring contextual meaning, and retaining multi-step verbal cues.

🔍 Observable Indicators (Home & School)
  • Needing multi-step instructions repeated several times before comprehension.
  • Difficulty identifying main themes, sequence of events, or implied subtext.
  • Can decode words accurately aloud but cannot summarize what was just read.
💡 Evidence-Based Supportive Strategies
  • Visual anchor charts, mind maps, and bulleted step-by-step checklists.
  • Reciprocal questioning: pausing after each section to summarize key takeaways.
  • Pre-teaching critical vocabulary and providing background concept primers.
🩺 When to Seek Consultation

Comprehension gaps persist across both spoken and written formats, impacting daily classroom task execution.

🔢
Domain 05 • Numerical Reasoning

Mathematics (Dyscalculia)

Understanding numerical concepts, arithmetic facts, spatial number lines, and mathematical symbols.

🔍 Observable Indicators (Home & School)
  • Difficulty estimating basic quantities, reading analog clocks, or counting money.
  • Struggling to recall foundational arithmetic tables and basic plus/minus operations.
  • Confusion aligning numbers vertically in columns for multidigit addition/division.
💡 Evidence-Based Supportive Strategies
  • Concrete math manipulatives (Base-10 blocks, fraction tiles, bead bars).
  • Visual color-coded grid paper to keep columns aligned neatly.
  • Formula reference sheets and calculator accommodations during problem solving.
🩺 When to Seek Consultation

Foundational number concepts remain ungrounded despite repeated multisensory tutoring and extra practice.

Important Contextual Guidance

These academic areas represent common manifestations of learning differences. A student experiencing difficulty in one of these areas does NOT automatically have a clinical disability. Difficulties can stem from instruction pace, linguistic transitions, or stress.

Important Educational Disclaimer: This portal is for educational awareness and guidance only. It does NOT provide clinical diagnosis, medical evaluation, or treatment. For formal diagnosis, always consult a qualified clinical psychologist, special educator, or developmental pediatrician.
