Module 4

Learner Support & Study Strategies

Practical, empowering techniques to master your study sessions, organize thoughts, and build lasting confidence.

1. Study Planning

Break your week into realistic, 30-minute study blocks. Color-code subjects and maintain a simple visual checklist on your desk.

2. Chunking Heavy Tasks

Never tackle a whole chapter at once. Read one heading, write two key takeaway bullets, then pause. Small victories prevent cognitive overload.

3. Active Reading Tools

Use colored highlighter overlays, ruler line-trackers, or free screen-readers (text-to-speech) to absorb textbooks effortlessly.

4. Structured Writing

Brainstorm with mind maps or voice notes first. Convert your spoken ideas into bullet points before writing full paragraphs.

5. Multi-Sensory Spelling

Trace challenging words on textured surfaces or air-write them while saying the phonetic sounds aloud. Visual rhythm builds memory.

6. Mathematics Strategies

Use graph paper to align columns cleanly. Utilize physical counters, color-coded arithmetic steps, and formula reminder cards.

7. Memory & Mnemonics

Create quirky rhymes, acronyms, and flashcards with sketches to remember complex scientific classifications or historical dates.

8. Time Management (Pomodoro)

Work for 25 focused minutes followed by a 5-minute break away from screens. Regular resets maintain high mental stamina.

9. Exam Prep & Accommodations

Practice with past papers under untimed conditions first. Understand your legal rights to extra time or a reader/scribe during board exams.

10. Scheduled Rest

Sleep is when your brain consolidates memory. Prioritize 8 hours of sleep and physical movement during intense study weeks.

11. Asking for Clarification

It is a sign of strength, not weakness, to tell your teacher: "Could you please explain that concept with an everyday example?"

12. Self-Advocacy

Learn to explain your learning strengths and the tools that help you succeed. Your voice shapes an inclusive classroom.
